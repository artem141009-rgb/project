Welcome to Albania!

Land of the Eagles and Ancient Wonders

Paradise Beaches

The Albanian Riviera is famous for its crystal blue water and long sandy beaches. It is one of the last "hidden treasures" of Europe where you can enjoy peace and beauty.

Secret Bays

Explore secluded bays with turquoise waters. These spots are perfect for boat trips, swimming, and enjoying the Mediterranean sun in a private atmosphere.

Wild Nature

Travel through deep canyons and river valleys. Albania's nature is diverse, offering breathtaking views that will make your trip unforgettable.

Charming Coastal Towns

Visit historic towns located right on the water. The combination of old European architecture and sea views creates a magical atmosphere for tourists.

Ancient Heritage

History is everywhere here. From Ottoman stone bridges to medieval castles, you can feel the spirit of the past in every corner of the country.

Vibrant Cities

When the sun goes down, Albanian cities come alive with lights. Enjoy the hospitality, local food, and great nightlife in Saranda or Vlora.
